What the audit covers

The checklist focuses on durable visibility fundamentals rather than unverified shortcuts or guaranteed-citation claims.

Crawlability & indexability

Confirm that important public content can be discovered, accessed, rendered and indexed.

AI crawler accessibility

Review crawler policies, bot protection and access controls intentionally.

Entity clarity

Make your organization, people, products and areas of expertise easy to understand.

Topical authority

Build meaningful coverage around priority subjects instead of isolated articles.

Answer-first content

Structure content so useful answers, definitions, comparisons and evidence are easy to extract.

Information gain

Add original expertise, examples, research, frameworks and first-party experience.

Citation readiness

Support important claims with verifiable evidence, methodology and credible sources.

Structured data

Use accurate schema markup to reinforce machine understanding where appropriate.

Third-party authority

Evaluate credible mentions, professional profiles, industry references and independent coverage.

Community presence

Learn from real audience questions and participate transparently in relevant communities.

Prompt monitoring

Track a stable set of informational, commercial and brand-related questions over time.

Measurement & governance

Document changes, referrals, mentions, citations, methodology and uncertainty.

A repeatable workflow

AI answer systems can vary from one response to another. Use a consistent process rather than judging visibility from a single prompt.

Discover→Benchmark→Audit→Improve→Validate→Monitor
Important: no single technical change, schema type, prompt trick or file guarantees inclusion in ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity or Google AI Overviews. Strong visibility usually depends on a combination of accessibility, usefulness, authority, evidence and external corroboration.

Simple scoring model

Use the score to prioritize work, not as a promise of AI mentions or citations.
